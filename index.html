Disciplan

A daily plan that works around your life. Disciplan builds a short plan each morning — usually one to five things — around your calendar, your energy and what you have actually been doing.

This page exists for the few things an app is required to publish. If you are looking for the app itself, it is in testing.

The short version of the privacy policy

  • We do not sell your data, and we never will.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking in Disciplan. No analytics company receives anything.
  • Nothing reaches another person unless you turn it on and they agree, and then only what you switched on.
  • You can see everything we hold, take a copy, or delete all of it, from inside the app.
