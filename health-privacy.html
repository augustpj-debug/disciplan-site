Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

details must be filled in.

This is the separate policy for data from Apple Health and Android Health Connect, which Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) requires to be its own document with its own link. Everything else Disciplan holds is in the Privacy Policy.

The same text is shown inside the app, at Profile → Health app → Consumer health data privacy policy.

What we collect

  • Workouts you have recorded: the kind of activity, folded into a short list of general kinds such as walking, running or strength work, and how many minutes it lasted.
  • Steps: a daily total.
  • Sleep: how many minutes you slept for the night that ended that morning.
  • Your answers in the app: your goal, the activities you do, your usual energy time, what gets in your way, and foods you avoid.
  • What you do in the app: which tasks you completed or skipped, and on which days.
  • The kind and timing of events on your calendar, if you connect one. We store that something is a game, a practice, a shift, travel, a holiday or an ordinary appointment, and when it runs. We do not store titles, locations, notes or who else is invited.
  • Check-in photos you choose to add, if you add any. A photo you take or pick after finishing something, and the day and the task it belongs to. We read nothing else from your camera or your photo library: not where a photo was taken, not when, and not any other photo.
  • Your inner circle, if you turn that on: who is in it, the invite codes you make, who you have blocked or reported, the cheers sent between you, and the meals you chose to share. A cheer is one of five fixed phrases; there are no messages in Disciplan.

Where it comes from

  • From you, when you answer a question or tick off a task.
  • From Apple Health or Android Health Connect, if you connect one and only after you have agreed on a separate screen.
  • From your device calendar, if you connect one.
  • From your camera or photo library, one photo at a time, when you choose to add a check-in photo.
  • From another person, only when you have both agreed: their first name, their count for the day, their streak, any photo they chose to share, and any meal they chose to share.
  • We do not buy health data, and we do not receive it from data brokers or other companies.

Why we collect it

  • To build your daily plan and keep it realistic.
  • To tick off work you have already done so you are not asked to do it twice.
  • To count the days you showed up, and to work out when you have earned a partner offer.
  • To send the reminders you asked for, which are scheduled on your own device.
  • To show somebody in your inner circle what you have chosen to share with them, if you have turned it on and they have accepted.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and we do not profile you for anything beyond your own plan.

Who we share it with

  • Nobody, with one narrow exception: Supabase, the company that hosts our database, stores it on our behalf under a contract that forbids any other use. They are a processor, not a recipient.
  • The people you choose, and only what you choose. Your inner circle is off until you turn it on, and adding somebody needs both people: one shares an invite code, the other has to accept it. They can then see your first name, how many things you finished today, your streak, the check-in photos you have shared and the meals you have shared - each of those behind its own switch, and photos and meals one at a time.
  • They can never see anything from Apple Health or Health Connect, any calendar event, the meals you pick for your own week, your training sessions, what you would rather avoid in a workout, your goals, or any answer you gave. Those are not hidden from a query: they are not in the two sets of rows another person is allowed to read.
  • Removing somebody, blocking them, or turning your inner circle off stops all of it immediately.
  • We do not share health data with partners, advertisers, analytics companies, insurers, employers or anyone else.
  • We do not sell health data, and we will not. Selling consumer health data requires separate written authorisation under the Act; we do not ask for one because we do not do it.
  • Partners who provide reward codes are told only that a code was redeemed.
  • We will disclose data if the law requires it, and we will tell you unless we are forbidden from doing so.

How long we keep it

  • Health figures are kept while your account is open and the connection is on. Disconnecting deletes them immediately.
  • Calendar-derived events are kept for the few days the plan needs and then removed.
  • Check-in photos are kept until you delete them or delete your account. They are held in private storage: there is no address that opens one without being signed in as you or as a friend you shared it with.
  • If you stop sharing a photo, it stops being shared straight away: your friends cannot see it, cannot open it and cannot be given a new link to it. One honest exception: for up to an hour, a friend who had already opened that photo may still be served the copy their own session fetched, from the cache our hosting provider keeps. They had already seen it; nobody new can.
  • Deleting your account deletes everything, including the photo files and every friendship, and including backups, within 30 days.

Your rights

  • You can ask us to confirm what health data we hold, and to give you a copy.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time, in Profile, under Health app. Disconnecting both withdraws consent and deletes what was collected.
  • You can ask us to delete your health data, and we will delete it from our systems and ask our processor to do the same.
  • You can ask who we have shared it with, and we will tell you, which for health data is nobody beyond our hosting processor.
  • We will answer within 45 days, and will tell you if we need longer.
  • If we refuse a request we will say why, and you may appeal by replying to our answer. If we turn down your appeal you may complain to the Washington State Attorney General.

How to reach us

  • Email: supportdisciplan@gmail.com
  • Postal address: available on request — please email us
  • We will ask you to confirm you are the account holder before we act on a request.

Children

Disciplan is for adults. We do not knowingly collect health data from anyone under 16, and we will delete it if we learn that we have.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or what we use it for, we will ask for your consent again rather than relying on the consent you gave for something else.
