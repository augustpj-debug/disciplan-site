What Disciplan is

Disciplan builds you a short daily plan — usually one to five things — around your real life: your calendar, your energy, and what you have actually been doing. It also holds meal ideas, stretching and breathing routines, a workout plan, and a way to share a little of it with a few people you choose.

By using Disciplan you agree to these terms. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the app.

What Disciplan is not

This is the most important section, so it is near the top.

  • Disciplan is not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent anything. It is general wellness guidance of the kind you would find in a book.
  • It is not a doctor, a physiotherapist, a dietitian or a trainer. Nobody qualified is looking at your plan.
  • Talk to a professional before you start, especially if you are pregnant, recovering from an injury or an operation, managing a condition, taking medication, or you have been told to be careful with exercise.
  • If something hurts, stop. Every workout in the app says so.
  • We do not count calories, set macro targets, or set weight goals, and we will not. Portions are described as plates and hands, never as numbers. If you want that kind of plan, Disciplan is the wrong app, and we would rather say so than pretend.

You exercise and eat at your own risk, and you are responsible for your own judgement about what is safe for you today.

Your account

  • You need to be 16 or over to have an account. The workout plan is for people 18 and over, and it asks your age for that reason.
  • Keep your sign-in details to yourself. Tell us if you think somebody else has got into your account.
  • One account per person.
  • You can delete your account at any time, from Profile → Privacy settings. It is a real deletion.

What you put in

You own what you write — the meals you add, your notes, your check-in photos. You keep owning them.

By putting them in Disciplan you give us permission to store them and show them back to you, and, only for the specific things you choose to share, to show them to the people in your inner circle. That permission ends when you unshare the thing or delete it.

What you may not do

  • Share anything you do not have the right to share.
  • Share anything obscene, hateful, harassing, threatening, or that sexualises anybody.
  • Share a photo of somebody who has not agreed to it.
  • Use Disciplan to advertise, to spam, or to sell anything.
  • Pretend to be somebody else.
  • Try to get at other people's data, or to get around the rules that keep it separate.
  • Take the app apart, copy its content wholesale, or scrape it.

We may remove anything that breaks these rules and close accounts that keep doing it. Every shared photo and every shared meal has a Report option on it, and a report is read by a person.

Other people

Your inner circle is off until you turn it on, and adding somebody needs both of you to agree. You decide what is visible, one switch and one item at a time, and you can stop at any point.

Be decent to the people in it. There are no messages in Disciplan — a cheer is one of five fixed phrases — which keeps most of the trouble out, but Block and Report are on every person for the rest.

Rewards from partners

Disciplan sometimes offers a discount code from a partner for turning up over time. When it does:

  • The offer is always labelled as a partner offer or sponsored.
  • The partner's own terms apply to the offer itself, and they decide whether to honour it. We are not the seller.
  • Partners are told only that a code was redeemed. They receive nothing about your health, your activity or your plan.
  • Codes can expire, run out, or be withdrawn by the partner.
  • We may change or stop the rewards at any time.

Content in the app

The recipes, routines, workouts, tasks and words in Disciplan are ours, or used with permission. They are for your own personal use. Please do not republish them, sell them, or build another product out of them.

Links you add

You can save a link to a recipe you cook. Only http and https links are accepted. What is at the other end is not ours and we have not checked it.

The app may change

We add things, change things, and sometimes take things away. Some features need permission from your phone — your calendar, notifications, your health data, your camera — and all of those are optional; the app works without them.

Some features need the internet, and some need a newer version of the app than the one on your phone. When a feature needs a newer version, the app says so rather than failing quietly.
