Support

Email us and a person will read it. We are two people, so it may take a day or two, but it will not go unread.

Email

supportdisciplan@gmail.com

What to include

  • What you were doing, and what you expected to happen instead.
  • A screenshot, if you have one. It is usually worth more than a description.
  • Your iPhone’s iOS version, if you know it. Settings → General → About.

Reporting somebody, or something they shared

Inside the app, everybody in your inner circle has Report and Block on them, and so does every photo or meal they have shared. A report goes to us and not to them, and nothing about you is sent with it.

We read reports within 24 hours. Blocking takes effect immediately and is invisible to the person blocked.

If you would rather email it, use the address above.

Your data

In the app, Profile → Privacy settings lets you see everything we hold, download a copy of it, or delete your account and all of its data. Deletion is real and permanent.

You can also email us to ask what we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We answer within 45 days and will tell you if we need longer.

Not medical advice

Disciplan offers general wellness guidance. It is not medical advice, it does not diagnose anything, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or a physiotherapist. If something hurts, stop.
